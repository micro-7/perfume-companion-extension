Sauvage Dior for men

Sauvage by Dior is a Aromatic Fougere fragrance for men. Sauvage was launched in 2015. The nose behind this fragrance is Francois Demachy.

Main accords: fresh spicy 100%, amber 72%, citrus 68%, aromatic 61%, musky 49%, woody 47%, lavender 44%

Perfume Pyramid

Top Notes

Middle Notes

Sichuan Pepper, Lavender, Pink Pepper, Vetiver, Patchouli, Geranium, Elemi

Base Notes

Ambroxan, Cedar, Labdanum
