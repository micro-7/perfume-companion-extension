Monsoon arrives early in Kerala, IMD says

The southwest monsoon reached the Kerala coast three days ahead of its normal date, the India Meteorological Department said on Saturday.

Heavy rainfall is expected over the next 48 hours in several districts. Fishermen have been advised not to venture into the sea.

Farmers welcomed the early arrival, which is expected to help kharif sowing across the southern states.
